# DevFest 2027 - homepage

Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve with `python3 -m http.server`.

## How it works
- **Hero** (`.hero`) is 240vh tall with a sticky 100vh viewport inside it. `script.js` turns scroll position into a CSS variable `--p` (0 = day, 1 = sunset).
- `--p` drives: the sunset skyline's opacity, the sunset sky fill (portrait), the blimp's horizontal position, and the scroll hint fading out.
- **Skyline art** is 16:9 (`skyline-day-wide.jpg` / `skyline-sunset-wide.jpg`, 3840x2160). **`.stage`** is sized and placed exactly like the displayed image; the variables that do it (`--art-w/-h/-left/-top`) are on `.stage` in `styles.css`. `.hero__sticky` is a CSS size container, so `cqw/cqh` = the visible window (no desktop scrollbar; `100dvh` on mobile).
  - **Landscape** (window wider than tall): the image **covers** the window, centered. No gaps, no blur. Squarer windows lose a bit of the outer left/right edges; the labeled buildings are at 22-74% of the width, so they stay visible down to 1:1 (1280x1024 shows 14.8%-85.2%).
  - Ultrawide windows lose sky and water, centered, but the crop is clamped so the labeled buildings stay fully visible: at most `--crop-top-max: 0.163` from the top (Tracks roof at 16.6%) and keep at least `--keep-bottom: 0.915` (building hotspots end at 91.1%). That works up to about 2.37:1 (21:9, e.g. 2560x1080). The antenna (tip at 5.7%) is cropped on screens wider than about 1.9:1.
  - **Portrait** (`@container (aspect-ratio < 1)`): covering would cut off most buildings, so the image fits the full width, anchored to the bottom, and `.sky--day/--sunset` fill the space above with the art's own top-edge colors (sampled left/middle/right).
- **Hotspots, boat and blimp live inside `.stage`** and are positioned in `%` of the art, so they always line up with the drawing. Hotspot positions (left/top/width/height) are in `styles.css`. Schedule overlaps About and comes later in the HTML, so it's on top. The hover tag shows inside the bottom of each building, so it's never cropped.
- Buildings -> `#about`, `#tracks`, `#schedule`, `#judges`, `#faq`. Boat -> `#sponsors`.
- **Blimp** (`--bw`, `--blimp-top` on `.stage`): 39% of the art wide, but at least 580px (so the two-line countdown fits at full size) and never wider than the window. On phones (under 600px wide) it's 85% of the screen (at least 320px). It flies from the center of the art (x 51%, just right of the Tracks tower) to the window's left edge as you scroll; the start is pulled left if the blimp would stick out past the window's right edge, so it's never cut off. Landscape: in the art's sky, 3% from the top (or just below the window's top when the sky is cropped), above the labeled buildings. Portrait: in the sky above the art, its bottom above the antenna tip. On ultrawide screens (sky cropped) it passes over the top of the Tracks tower mid-scroll, above the "TRACKS" lettering.
- **Countdown on the blimp** (`script.js`, "Blimp countdown"): counts down to **2027-02-06T09:00:00-05:00** (Feb 6, 2027, 9:00 AM New York time), an exact moment, so it's right in every time zone. The banner shows "Feb 6, 2027" on top (`clamp(18px, 3.1cqw, 28px)`) and "89 days 04 hrs 12 min" below (`clamp(28px, 4.9cqw, 46px)`), bold, in the ink color. On a phone-size blimp (under 580px wide) it switches to one line, "89d 04h 12m" (`clamp(18px, 8.2cqw, 34px)`). `.blimp` is a size container, so `cqw` = % of the blimp's width and the text always fits the cream banner. It updates just after every whole second, so the minute ticks over on time. From the start it shows "Happening now!", and from Feb 9 (after Feb 8) "Thanks for coming!". The visible text is `aria-hidden`; a visually hidden `aria-live="polite"` element (`.countdown-live`) says the same in words and only changes once a minute.
- **Boat** (`--boat-w`, `--bdrift` on `.stage`; links to `#sponsors`): `clamp(140px, 14vw, 300px)` wide (vw = window), capped at 4.6x the visible water strip so the waterline always reaches at least halfway up the hull (only matters on ultrawide screens: about 219px at 2560x1080, where just a 48px strip of water shows). Phones (under 600px wide): 28% of the screen. Everything is measured against the visible window, not the (possibly cropped) art:
  - bottom: just above the window's bottom edge (or 1% up from the art's bottom when that's visible). The hull is 59-92% down the image; the bob (4% down) and rock stay inside the image's transparent bottom margin, so the boat is never cut off.
  - left: 6% of the window, pulled left if the hull's tip (plus drift) would reach the Schedule hotspot, but never so far that the drift or hover pushes it off the left edge. On phones that means ~2% from the left with a small drift (2% of its width instead of 8%).
  - Three loops with unrelated durations: `boat-drift` (side to side, 13.7s), `boat-bob` (up/down, 3.3s), `boat-rock` (tilt, 5.1s). Hover: grows and tilts.
  - Only the drawn boat is clickable (the img has a `clip-path` traced from the art and the link itself has `pointer-events: none`), so the image's transparent corners never block a building hotspot behind it.
- **Sponsors** (`#sponsors`) is the underwater section: `sea.jpg` (the sea painting stacked twice), 7 fish, resting bubbles and the sponsor tiers (`.tier--gold/silver/bronze`).
  - **Layout**: the fish swim in the top 64vw; the heading and logos follow in normal flow (`padding-top: 64vw`), and the section grows to fit them (`min-height: 112.7vw`, the Figma frame). Logo widths: gold `clamp(180px, 24vw, 420px)` (on phones under 600px: at least 60% of the screen), silver `clamp(130px, 16vw, 300px)`, bronze `clamp(90px, 11vw, 200px)`. Several logos in a tier wrap onto centered rows. Heading `clamp(2.8rem, 7vw, 6rem)`.
  - **Fish** (`.fish` wrapper + `img`): the wrapper crosses at a steady, linear speed (10-22s, `--t`), entering off one side and leaving off the other. The img does a long smooth wave (3vw tall, one full wave = 2 x `--wh` = 4-8s, ease-in-out), tilts nose-up while rising and nose-down while falling (in sync with the wave, a quarter-wave behind so the tilt peaks when it moves fastest), and has a tiny 0.6s horizontal squash/stretch for a tail-beat feel. Everything is transform-only. Settings are inline CSS vars in `index.html` (`--y`, `--w`, `--t`, `--delay`, `--wh`, `--wd`, `--flip`, `--x`). The art faces LEFT for fish-1/4/5/6 and RIGHT for fish-2/7/8; `--flip:-1` mirrors a fish swimming the other way. Fish stay above the heading.
  - **Resting bubbles**: 47, in `BUBBLES` in `script.js` as `[x, y, size]` in vw on the Figma frame (the original 17 plus 30 in overlapping "streams"). `layoutBubbles()` stretches the part below the heading (y > 64) to the section's real height, then pushes any bubble that could touch the heading or a logo (including its bob and scroll drift) sideways, so the middle stays clear. Bubbles are also kept fully above the sea's bottom edge (with their bob and drift), so the FAQ below never slices one in half. See-through (opacity 0.75-0.85), small ones in front of big ones (z-index), they float up a little and bob, drift with scroll (parallax, only while the section is on screen), and pop on click. They live in `.bubbles` (z-index 1) under `.sponsors` (z-index 2), so the heading and logos are on top and clickable.
  - **"Bubble up, then clear" transition** (`.sea[data-phase]`, `script.js`):
    1. `armed`: resting bubbles and sponsors hidden.
    2. When ~30% of the section is on screen (capped at 60% of the window, since this section can be taller than the screen), `burst`: 40-60 temporary `.burst-bubble`s (2-25vw; scaled up on tall phone screens so they still fill it) rise from below the visible part, staggered over ~0.3s, with a sideways wobble, overlapping, so at the peak (~0.8s) ~85-95% of the visible section is covered, logos included. They are above everything (z-index 4) but `pointer-events: none`. Animated with the Web Animations API, transform + opacity only.
    3. They keep rising out the top and fade (about 30% pop like a click) and are removed. Any one spot (e.g. a logo) is covered for under ~1s.
    4. At 0.95s, `settled`: resting bubbles pop in with a little bounce (staggered) and the heading + logos fade/scale up. Everything is clear by ~2s.
    5. When the section fully leaves the screen it resets to `armed`, so the burst replays next time.
  - `.sea` has `overflow: hidden`, so off-screen fish and bubbles never cause sideways scrolling.
- **Reduced motion** (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`): no bubble burst (no `data-phase`, the final layout is shown), no swimming, bobbing, drifting or parallax; fish rest at their `--x` spots; the FAQ bear stays put (it still jumps to the current question), answers open instantly, the light shafts hold still, the specks and reef bubbles are hidden, and there's no bubble trip (the bear just sits by its question). The countdown still updates.
- **FAQ** (`#faq`, `.reef`) is the reef under the Sponsors: `faq-reef.webp` sits at the bottom, full width (`--art-h: 53.89vw`, the art's aspect). The section is at least a full screen tall and always has a band of open water above the painting (`clamp(160px, 18vw, 340px)`); it grows with the questions. The questions start 21vw from the top (a fixed spot, not centered, so opening an answer never moves the question you clicked). The Sponsors sea fades to one flat teal at its bottom (`.sea::after`, `--sea-join`); the FAQ's water starts from that same teal and deepens to the painting's top color (`--reef-top`) exactly where the art begins, so there are no seams.
  - **Questions** (`.faq__item`): each bar is a 9-slice `border-image` of the chalk art (`faq-pill.webp` closed, `faq-pill-hover.webp` on hover, `faq-pill-open.webp` open, with the minus), so the middle stretches to fit the text and the round ends and +/- button keep their shape. `--k` (0.48, phones 0.44) scales the art. The answer opens joined to its question (`grid-template-rows: 0fr -> 1fr`), one at a time: the panel (`faq-panel-joined.webp`) slides out from under the bar, its top half tucked behind it (`--tuck`), so bar and panel read as one piece. Numbers come from a CSS counter. To add a question, copy an `<li>` and give its answer a new id.
  - Desktop: the bear (`clamp(130px, 13vw, 230px)`) + numbers + bars (`min(30vw, 520px)`) are centered in the open water, then nudged 4vw to the left (`left: -4vw`), which runs from 24% to 79% of the art. Under 760px wide (phones) the questions take the full width below the sign, the section is only as tall as it needs to be (no full-screen minimum) with the reef right under the questions, the bear is smaller and the bars slimmer (`--k` 0.38) so each question fits on one line, the far-off fish are hidden, and the bear's bubble rides lower, across the edge, clear of the sponsor logos.
  - **Twilight**: `.reef__light` = 4 warm, blurred light shafts slanting down from the surface (`mix-blend-mode: screen`, so they only brighten; each breathes over 9s), and `.reef__motes` = 18 tiny glowing specks drifting up (made by `script.js`, seeded so they're the same every visit). Both sit behind the sign and the questions. The bars' blue fill is about 50% see-through (the chalk rim and +/- stay solid) with a 3px frosted-glass blur, so the water and light show through; the number circles match.
  - **Sea -> reef transition**: besides the color handoff, 6 small bubbles (3.5-7.5vw) rise out of the reef, across the edge and up into the Sponsors sea, fading out as they go. Same trick as the big bubbles between the sea and the subway: each is drawn twice, identically, in `.reef-rise--reef` and `.reef-rise--sea` (`--rr-in` 34vw of reef + `--rr-up` 38vw of sea), so it reads as one bubble crossing over. Behind the questions.
  - **The bear's trip** (`script.js`, "The bear's trip"; `.bear-trip` in CSS): a stand-in bear in a bubble (`bubble.png` over `faq-bear.webp`, the bubble 1.5x the bear) rides in a page-level layer over the sea and the reef, while the real `.faq__bear` is hidden (`.is-away`). As you scroll down from the Sponsors, it swims from off the left edge to 46% across, nearly level, low across the edge between the sections (clear of the sponsor logos), bobbing gently. This is tied to the scroll (p = 0 -> 1 as the FAQ's top edge climbs from the bottom of the window to 40% down it), so it drifts back if you scroll up. At p = 1 (once per visit) the bubble pops: it wobbles (squash and stretch, 170ms), then bursts: a bright flash, a ring rushing out (`.bear-trip__ring`), 10 short chalky pop strokes (`.bear-trip__spark`) and 9 droplets; small bubbles stream up off the bear as it dives (`puff()`, more the faster it moves); the bear turns to face straight down (the art faces right, so +80deg), swims down to question 1 and levels out (2s), landing exactly where the real bear sits, which then takes over. **Then the questions appear**: while the bear travels, `.faq.is-hidden` hides the questions and the path; on landing, the dashed line draws itself along its length (a mask path's `stroke-dashoffset`, `#faq-path-mask`): number 1 pops in where the bear lands (`.is-dot`), then its question fades in (`.is-in`). Then **the bear swims the line down**, its lantern riding the tip of the line as it draws (1.3s per stretch), and each number pops in and its question fades in as the bear reaches it. About 10s for five questions; then the bear follows your mouse. Hidden questions can't be clicked, and tabbing into them shows them at once. Skipped if the page opens already past that point (e.g. a link to `#faq`) or with reduced motion.
  - **Swaying seaweed**: from the Procreate file (`~/Downloads/FAQ.procreate`): its 8 seaweed clumps (each a base layer + its clipped shading) and the 2 pairs of long vines hanging off the arch (split out of the moss layer) are `assets/weed/weed-1..8.webp` and `vine-1..2.webp`; `faq-reef.webp` is the exported painting with the spots behind them rebuilt from the other layers (color-matched to the export; the bear sketch on the bottom layer erased). They sit back in place in `.reef__art` (`--l/--t/--w` = % of the art) and lean side to side from where they grow (`skewX` around `--oy`, 2.4-3.2deg, 4.6-7.1s each, out of step; the vines swing from the top). `assets/weed/front.webp` is a still overlay of what's in front of the seaweed in the drawing (shells, pearls, coral, moss dabs on the stone), drawn above the swaying pieces.
  - **A fish drawn to the lantern**: when the bear stays still for about 3s, a small fish (`fish-2.png`, `.lure-fish`, in front of the bear) swims over to its light and nibbles at it, then darts off with a few bubbles as soon as the bear moves (or after about 5s). 7s before the next one. Not with reduced motion.
  - **Tap the water**: clicking or tapping open water in the FAQ sends up a little burst of bubbles (not on the questions, links, pearls or sign, which have their own taps). Not with reduced motion.
  - **Background life**: two small, dim, soft schools of the Sponsors fish drift across far back in the open water above the questions (`.reef__fish`, 9% and 18% down the section, 52s / 64s a crossing); the two painted pearls glow and twinkle (`.reef__art` is laid exactly over the painting, so `.pearl` positions are % of the art: 72.06/85.86 and 17.08/97.43) and flare with a puff of bubbles when you point at or tap one; the bear's lantern throws a soft warm pool of light around it (`.faq__pool`). The sign is let down on its ropes from the surface with a bounce the first time the FAQ comes into view (if it starts off screen). With reduced motion: no fish or drop-in; the pearls and lantern glow but don't pulse.
  - **Bubble trail**: whenever the bear swims (the dive, leading the line down, following your mouse), little groups of bubbles (`.puff`: mostly 6-12px, some 12-22px, now and then 22-36px; bigger ones float further and slower) drift up off it and fade, more the faster it moves. They sit behind the questions (`.faq__trail`). None with reduced motion.
  - **Bear** (`faq-bear.webp`, mirrored so it faces the questions) swims **along the dashed line** (`script.js`, "The bear swims along the line"): to the height of your mouse anywhere over the FAQ section (a gentle eased follow, tilting as it moves up or down), else the question you tabbed to, else the open one, else the first. Its lantern (82.3% / 16.7% of the art) sits just left of the line, and the number it's next to lights up gold. Touch screens have no hover, so there it swims to the question you tap. With reduced motion it jumps straight there, no tilt. **Dashed path** (`.faq__line`): `script.js` ("FAQ: the reef") redraws it whenever the list changes size: S-curves from number 1 down through each number in turn.
  - **Sign** (`.reef__sign`, `sign-faq.png`): top left over the ruins, `clamp(220px, 25vw, 440px)` wide (phones: `clamp(180px, 52vw, 300px)`), its ropes fading up into the water (mask), as if they hang from far above; hangs in a gentle current (`sign-float`: a 7s sway with a little bob). Every 2-4s a tiny bubble or two rises from the bubbles drawn by its "Q" (only while it's on screen), and pointing at it (or tapping it) makes it swing on its ropes and settle (1.9s damped swing) with a puff of bubbles. None of that with reduced motion. The light shafts are masked like the ropes, so nothing marks the join with the sea.
- **Footer**: just "DevFest 2027", on the reef floor's dark color.
